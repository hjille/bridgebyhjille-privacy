Bridge Night is a bridge card game for iPhone and iPad. You can play locally on your device or use online features powered by Apple's Game Center and, for some matches, iCloud/CloudKit.

Local and offline play

Local play does not require Game Center. If you experience problems with saved local games, settings, language or resuming a game, please describe what happened and whether you can reproduce the issue.

Online play with Game Center

Online features use Game Center for sign-in, player identity, friend lists, invitations and turn-based matches. If an online feature is not working, check that you are signed in to Game Center in your iOS/iPadOS settings.

If an invitation or turn-based match seems stuck, try reopening the app, checking your network connection and seeing whether the match appears in Game Center or the app's match list.

Friend invitations and turn-based matches

To invite friends, the app may need permission to access your Game Center friends list. If your friends do not appear, check Game Center permissions and restrictions in your device settings.

Turn-based matches are updated through Game Center and may also use iCloud/CloudKit for match data. Synchronization can sometimes take a little time depending on your network connection and Apple's services.

Report a problem

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Whether you were playing locally or online.
  • Which iPhone or iPad you are using.
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The app version and build number, if known.

If you have enabled diagnostic sharing in the app, technical diagnostics may help with troubleshooting. Diagnostic sharing is optional and is described in the privacy policy.

Help with personal data

If you have questions about personal data, CloudKit match data, diagnostics or would like help requesting deletion, contact support@hjille.se. If your question concerns online play, please indicate the relevant Game Center identity or match where possible.
