About the app

Bridge Night is a bridge card game for iPhone and iPad. You can play locally on your device, while online features use Apple's Game Center for player identity, friends, invitations and turn-based matches.

Some online matches use Apple's iCloud/CloudKit to synchronize match data and results. Diagnostic sharing is optional and can be enabled or disabled in the app's settings.
