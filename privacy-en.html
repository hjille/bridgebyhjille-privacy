Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Bridge by Hjille handles information when you play locally, use Game Center, play online through iCloud/CloudKit, or choose to share diagnostics.

Bridge by Hjille does not require an account with Hjille. You can play locally on your device. Some online features use Apple services such as Game Center, iCloud/CloudKit, and APNs.

1. Who Provides the App

Bridge by Hjille is provided by Eric Gille. Questions about the app, privacy, or support can be sent to support@hjille.se.

2. Local and Offline Play

You can play Bridge by Hjille locally on your iPhone or iPad without signing in to Game Center. In that case, information needed for app functionality is stored locally on the device, such as language choice, settings, local player name, saved games, game events, and local match statistics.

Locally saved games and settings are not sent to Hjille simply because you play offline. They may, however, be included in optional diagnostics if you have enabled diagnostic sharing.

3. Game Center, Player Identity, and Friends

When you use online features, the app uses Apple Game Center for sign-in, player identity, friend lists, invitations, turn-based matches, match results, and leaderboards.

The app may process your Game Center display name, Game Center player ID, match participants, whose turn it is, match status, and results. This information is needed to identify players, connect players to seats, pass turns, and display matches correctly.

If you choose to invite friends, the app may request access to your Game Center friend list. The friend list is used to show selectable friends and create a Game Center invitation. This feature is optional and only needed for friend invitations.

Game Center is operated by Apple. Apple’s own terms and privacy policy apply to Apple’s processing of information in Game Center.

4. Online Matches and Match History

When you play online, match state and history may be sent through Game Center and/or CloudKit. This may include match IDs, participants, player IDs, display names, seat assignments, settings, deal information, bids, played cards, scores, results, and other game history.

This information is used so both players can play the same match, resume a match, view results, and handle turn-based events.

5. iCloud and CloudKit

For some online matches, Bridge by Hjille uses Apple CloudKit in iCloud to synchronize match data. The app uses the CloudKit container iCloud.hjillesoft.Bridge-by-Hjille.

CloudKit may store match metadata, participant data, Game Center match IDs, player identifiers, display names, match settings, number of boards, status, per-board results, scores, and game history. Optional diagnostics may also be uploaded to CloudKit if you have enabled them.

CloudKit is operated by Apple. Data in the app’s CloudKit container may be accessible to the developer or development team for administration, troubleshooting, and support. This policy does not claim that CloudKit data is accessible only to the developer.

6. Optional Diagnostics

Diagnostic sharing is off by default. If you enable diagnostics in the app’s settings, the app may save and upload diagnostic data to CloudKit to help find and fix issues.

Diagnostics may include app version, build number, iOS/iPadOS version, device model, device type, whether the app is running in a simulator, build configuration, error descriptions, game state, recent game events, DDS calls, and technical information about AI analysis.

If you turn diagnostics off, the app clears pending diagnostic files on the device that have not yet been uploaded. Diagnostics that have already been uploaded are not automatically deleted simply because you turn diagnostic sharing off.

7. Push Notifications for Match Updates

The app may register for silent push notifications through Apple APNs/CloudKit. The purpose is to detect online match changes faster. The app does not depend on push notifications to work, because it also refreshes match data when opened, activated, and synchronized.

The app does not store an APNs token in its own code according to the implementation reviewed.

8. Third-Party Services

Bridge by Hjille uses Apple services for distribution and online features, including the App Store, TestFlight, iOS/iPadOS, Game Center, iCloud, CloudKit, and APNs. Apple’s own terms and privacy policy apply to Apple’s processing.

The app does not use external advertising services, analytics SDKs, or tracking scripts on this website.

9. Retention, Deletion, and Support Requests

Local information remains on your device as long as the app or the locally saved data remains there. Deleting the app normally removes the app’s local data from the device.

Game Center data is handled by Apple. You can manage Game Center and iCloud in iOS/iPadOS Settings.

For CloudKit match data and already uploaded diagnostics, no automatic retention period has been confirmed in the app implementation. If you want to ask about, get help with, or request deletion of information that may be connected to you, contact support@hjille.se.

10. Your Choices

  • You can play locally without Game Center.
  • You choose whether to use Game Center and online features.
  • You choose whether to allow access to your Game Center friend list for invitations.
  • You can turn diagnostic sharing on or off in the app’s settings.
  • You can contact support if you need help with privacy questions or deletion requests.

11. Tracking, Advertising, and Sale

Bridge by Hjille does not use information to track you across apps or websites. The app does not sell user data to third parties and contains no advertising according to the implementation reviewed.

12. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when app features, technical implementation, or legal requirements change. The latest version is published on this page.

13. Contact

Questions about Bridge by Hjille, support, or privacy can be sent to support@hjille.se.
